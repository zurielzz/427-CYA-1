# Choose Your Adventure: Full-Stack Sports Media & Analytics Opportunity

## Career Goals

**Short-term:** Graduate from Washington State University with a Computer Science degree and transition into a full-time role where I can combine my technical skills with my passion for soccer and sports analytics, and/or do a masters degree in Spain. 

**Long-term:** Establish myself as a full-stack developer specializing in sports media technology. I want to build and sell data pipelines and media products that serve soccer communities worldwide while growing as an engineer within the sports tech industry.

## The Adventure

My chosen adventure is securing and beginning work on a full-stack developer opportunity with a London-based sports media company that has over 4 million followers across social media. This opportunity came about organically through my personal project [madridstats.com](https://madridstats.com), a soccer analytics website I built independently. While I was applying for internships on LinkedIn like most students, the founder of this media company reached out to me directly on Twitter after discovering my work. Since then we have been planning and scoping out the website and soccer data pipeline I will be building for their platform.

Beyond this role I have also been developing a standalone product that uses AI agents to deliver real-time news updates to entities about their team of choice. This product represents my broader ambition to build sellable pipelines and media tools in the sports analytics space.

## How the Opportunity Came About

I had been building [madridstats.com](https://madridstats.com) as a personal project to sharpen my full-stack development skills and explore soccer analytics. The site caught the attention of the founder of a major sports media company based in London. He reached out to me on Twitter and after several conversations we began collaborating on plans for a new website and a soccer analytics data pipeline for his organization. This was not something I applied for through a job board. It came directly from putting my work out there and letting the quality of the product speak for itself.

## Prior Professional Experience

Before this sports fullstack/media opportunity I held two other roles at WSU that shaped how I work with teams and communicate professionally. From August 2024 through January 2025 I worked as an IT Technician where I responded to support tickets and worked directly with patrons to troubleshoot and resolve their issues. From August 2025 through the present I have been working as an Intramural Referee at UREC where I manage games and make real-time decisions under pressure. Both of these roles taught me leadership and teamwork and gave me experience communicating clearly with people in high-stakes or time-sensitive situations. Those skills have carried directly into how I interact with the founder of the media company and how I approach collaboration on technical projects.
