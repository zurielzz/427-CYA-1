# Reflection

## Activity Description

I secured a full-stack developer role with a London-based sports media company that has over 4 million followers. My responsibility involves building their website and developing a soccer data pipeline to power their platform. Alongside this I have been developing my own product(FootNews) that uses AI agents to deliver real-time team news to subscribers. The combination of this professional engagement and my independent product work represents well over the expected time commitment for this assignment.

## Technical and Professional Decisions

The most significant decision I made was investing time into building madridstats.com without any guarantee it would lead to anything. I chose technologies I wanted to grow in rather than building something safe for a portfolio. When the founder reached out on Twitter I focused on understanding the company's needs and showing how my existing work could translate into solutions for their platform. I also decided to keep developing my AI news agent product independently because it could become a sellable pipeline regardless of whether this specific role worked out.

## Contributions

The technical work has been entirely independent. I built madridstats.com on my own and I have been the sole developer scoping and planning the new platform with the company founder. The AI news agent product is also my own creation from concept to implementation. That said my ability to communicate and collaborate effectively did not come out of nowhere. Working as an IT Technician at WSU from August 2024 through January 2025 taught me how to respond to tickets professionally and talk with patrons to resolve issues on the spot. My current role as an Intramural Referee at UREC since August 2025 has strengthened my leadership and my ability to make decisions under pressure while working as part of a team. Those experiences directly shaped how I communicate with the founder and approach this collaboration.

## Quality Assessment

I am proud of how this played out but there are areas I would improve. My initial response to the founder could have been more polished and I should have had a structured proposal ready earlier. If I were to repeat this I would prepare a technical proposal template so I can present my vision professionally from the start. I would also document my development process more thoroughly along the way rather than retroactively. The technical work has been strong but my communication cadence could be more consistent.

## Career Alignment

This experience is directly aligned with everything I want to do professionally. Working as a full-stack developer in sports media and analytics is exactly the intersection of my skills and interests. This opportunity coming through my own project rather than a traditional application validates my approach of building things I care about and putting them out in the world. I am walking away from this with a clearer picture of what I want my career to look like and proof that building in public can open doors that cold applications on LinkedIn never would.
